## Patrick W. Davis

Commercial and marketing leader with 25+ years driving revenue growth, brand strategy, and demand generation across luxury mountain resort destinations and multi-property portfolios. Based in Vail, Colorado.

Full biography and background: **[patrickwdavis.com](https://patrickwdavis.com)**
